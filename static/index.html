How MediRAG works

Every answer is retrieved from your own medical documents — not hallucinated from thin air.

1

Document ingestion

Medical PDFs are parsed, chunked into passages, and stored in a local FAISS vector database.

2

Semantic search

Your question is embedded and matched against the most relevant passages using cosine similarity.

3

Grounded generation

The retrieved passages are injected into the prompt and a local LLM generates a precise, sourced answer.

4

History saved

Every conversation is stored per user so you can revisit and continue previous sessions anytime.
